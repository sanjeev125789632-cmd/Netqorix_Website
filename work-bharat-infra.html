Case Study — Renewable Infrastructure

Bharat Infra Company

Driving commercial solar inquiries and local map search visibility across regional markets.

SECTOR: SOLAR INFRASTRUCTURE

Context & Challenge

Bharat Infra Company executes commercial and residential solar projects. They needed a platform to showcase completed installations, capture project quote requests, and establish top rankings on Google Business Profile and Maps searches.

What We Built

  • Project Showcase Gallery for rooftop, industrial, and ground-mounted solar installations.
  • Interactive Solar Quote Estimator form routing requests directly to management.
  • Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, and geotagged project posts.

What Changed

Bharat Infra gained primary local visibility for regional solar searches, resulting in consistent inbound commercial quote requests.

"They understood solar as a business, not just a website brief. Project pages, quote requests, and a Google Business Profile that finally shows up in local searches — handled end to end."
MD Bundan Khan Managing Director, Bharat Infra Company

Client, sector and operating context

Bharat Infra Company is presented as a solar-infrastructure business serving commercial and residential projects. The live experience separates rooftop, industrial and ground-mounted installations, allowing a prospective buyer to move from a broad solar requirement to a relevant project category.

The engagement combines a project showcase with quote capture and local-search visibility. Those elements fit a considered purchase: visitors can inspect completed work, describe a proposed installation and contact the business through a route designed for project enquiries.

The problem before the build

The requirement documented on this page was to showcase installations, collect structured quote requests and strengthen visibility through Google Business Profile and Maps. The exact condition of the previous website and the business reason for starting the rebuild have not been supplied.

What was built

The delivered scope includes a project gallery organised around rooftop, industrial and ground-mounted solar work. This gives the business a visual proof layer and provides visitors with reference points before they request a quote.

An interactive solar quote estimator routes project requests to management. The site therefore does more than publish company information: it creates a structured path from browsing relevant installations to submitting a commercial requirement. Google Business Profile optimisation, local citations and geotagged project posts are also listed in the existing project record.

The observable page structure prioritises service explanations, project evidence and enquiry actions. That combination supports both informational searches and visitors who already know they need a solar proposal.
