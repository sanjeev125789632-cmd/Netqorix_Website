Case Study — Renewable Infrastructure

Bharat Infra Company

Driving commercial solar inquiries and local map search visibility across regional markets.

SECTOR: SOLAR INFRASTRUCTURE

Context & Challenge

Bharat Infra Company executes commercial and residential solar projects. They needed a platform to showcase completed installations, capture project quote requests, and establish top rankings on Google Business Profile and Maps searches.

What We Built

  • Project Showcase Gallery for rooftop, industrial, and ground-mounted solar installations.
  • Interactive Solar Quote Estimator form routing requests directly to management.
  • Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, and geotagged project posts.

What Changed

Bharat Infra gained primary local visibility for regional solar searches, resulting in consistent inbound commercial quote requests.

"They understood solar as a business, not just a website brief. Project pages, quote requests, and a Google Business Profile that finally shows up in local searches — handled end to end."
MD Bundan Khan Managing Director, Bharat Infra Company
