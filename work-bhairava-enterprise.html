Case Study — Industrial Engineering

Bhairava Enterprise

Complex industrial services transformed into a clear digital experience for technical buyers.

SECTOR: INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING

Context & Challenge

Bhairava Enterprise needed a professional website that could explain a broad industrial offering without forcing customers to interpret disconnected technical information. Hydraulic systems, electrical automation, pipeline work and traded components each required a clear route, while project buyers needed a simple way to submit specifications.

What We Built

  • Dedicated service paths for hydraulics, electrical systems, pipeline work and trading items.
  • Technical product and capability presentation designed for industrial buyers.
  • Responsive navigation and enquiry forms for submitting project requirements.
  • A focused contact journey with direct quotation and WhatsApp options.

What Changed

Customers can now explore engineering capabilities, browse relevant technical solutions and move directly to a project-requirement form. The completed website was delivered in under 10 days.

"Netqorix provided excellent service and delivered our website in under 10 days. We truly appreciate their professionalism, responsiveness, and commitment to the agreed timeline."
Sashi Bhairava Enterprise

Client and operating context

Bhairava Enterprise is an industrial engineering business based in Bangalore. Its public website presents hydraulic power packs and service, electrical control-panel and automation work, industrial pipeline installation, and component trading for manufacturing customers.

The offer covers distinct technical buying journeys. A customer looking for a hydraulic system needs different specifications and evidence from one seeking PLC, HMI, SCADA or VFD work, pipeline fabrication, or industrial components. The website therefore separates these capabilities instead of presenting them as one generic service list.

The information-architecture problem

The central design challenge was clarity. The website had to help visitors quickly identify the relevant engineering category, understand the available solutions and reach the correct enquiry action. The content also needed to remain usable on mobile devices, where technical buyers may be reviewing capabilities from a plant, workshop or project location.

Service and solution structure

The completed website gives hydraulics, electrical services, pipeline work and trading items their own navigation routes. The homepage introduces the overall industrial offer, establishes the Bangalore operating context and directs visitors into the appropriate capability page.

Technical examples visible on the website include AC and DC hydraulic power packs, tipper packs, cylinder repair and oil-filtration services; PLC, HMI and SCADA control panels; VFDs and servo motors; pipeline project work; and traded industrial components. This structure allows a buyer to narrow the discussion before submitting an enquiry.

Project-requirement journey

Quotation actions are placed alongside the capability content rather than being isolated in the footer. Visitors can submit technical specifications or describe a project requirement through the website, with direct contact and WhatsApp options available for urgent discussions.

The form language is aligned with industrial enquiries, asking for a company or requirement and allowing the buyer to describe technical specifications. This makes the first contact more useful than a generic message field and helps Bhairava Enterprise understand the nature of the request.

Delivery and result

The website launched on 28 August 2026 and was delivered in under 10 days. The approved client testimonial records excellent service, responsiveness, professionalism and commitment to the agreed timeline.

No unsupported traffic, ranking, enquiry-volume or revenue metric is claimed. The measurable result retained here is the confirmed delivery period; the functional outcome is the live, responsive pathway from engineering capability discovery to project-requirement submission.
