Case Study — Global Trade & AI

S S EXIM

Rapid cloud infrastructure deployment, SSL security compliance, and zero-friction handover.

SECTOR: GLOBAL TRADE & AI

Context & Challenge

S S EXIM required an expedited deployment of their international portal with strict security protocols, automated SSL auto-renewals, and cloud server provisioning ahead of a major trade deadline.

What We Built

  • Cloud Server Architecture on high-availability infrastructure.
  • Automated SSL/TLS certificate renewal pipelines.
  • Fixed-scope contract execution delivered on schedule without revision loops.

What Changed

S S EXIM launched seamlessly without technical hiccups or post-launch follow-up requirements.

"Deployment, SSL, and hosting were sorted without a single follow-up from our side. Clear scope, fixed quote, delivered on the committed timeline."
CEO Arun Kumar Chief Executive Officer, S S EXIM

Client, sector and operating context

S S EXIM operates from Thane in the Mumbai region and serves Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore and pan-India customers. Its live site presents the company as an electrical-products distributor, IoT and Building Management Systems integrator, and custom-panel manufacturer.

The published product scope includes IBMS and BMS 4.0 solutions, smart energy meters, IoT gateways and controllers, sensors, custom electrical panels, industrial switchgear and industrial LED lighting. The site also presents engineering services, a project gallery, brands, articles and a direct quote path.

The problem before the build

The existing Netqorix project record describes an expedited deployment with security, SSL renewal and cloud provisioning requirements. It also refers to a trade deadline. Further background about the previous website or the operational reason for that deadline has not been supplied.

What was built

The live website organises a broad technical catalogue into product and solution routes. Visitors can move from IBMS and energy monitoring to gateways, panels, switchgear and lighting, then continue to engineering services, brands, project evidence or a quote request.

The existing project record identifies high-availability cloud infrastructure and automated SSL/TLS certificate renewal pipelines as delivered components. The live site adds a structured product hub, individual solution routes, project-gallery access, editorial content and clear contact information.

The company’s service area and registered-office details are visible on the live site, along with its position as a Tor.AI channel partner and a distributor for listed electrical brands. Those are client-published facts; no additional partnership or performance claim is added here.

Deployment and handover

The published case study records automated SSL renewal, cloud server provisioning, fixed-scope execution and delivery on the committed schedule. It also records a launch without technical hiccups or post-launch follow-up requirements.

The result

The retained result is limited to the statement already on this page: the site launched seamlessly without technical hiccups or post-launch follow-up requirements.
