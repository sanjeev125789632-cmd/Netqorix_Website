Case Study — Professional Services

Alok S Kumar & Company

Establishing digital authority and qualified client acquisition for a premier Chartered Accountancy practice.

SECTOR: CHARTERED ACCOUNTANCY

Context & Challenge

Alok S Kumar and Company required a digital overhaul. Their existing website lacked clear service structure for audit, GST compliance, and corporate tax advisory. Unfiltered contact forms generated frequent automated spam while failing to convert high-value corporate inquiries.

What We Built

  • Structured Service Architecture for Audit, Tax Advisory, and Statutory Compliance.
  • Clean, lightweight HTML5 & CSS3 design system built without heavy frameworks.
  • Spam-filtered Lead Capture Form with client validation & honeypot mechanisms.
  • Technical schema markup for Professional Services and local search optimization.

What Changed

Site load time dropped under 0.8 seconds. Corporate inquiries increased in quality, providing direct clarity on client requirements prior to initial discovery calls.

"We needed a site that looked as credible as our practice. Netqorix delivered a clean, fast website with proper service pages, and the enquiry form now brings us qualified client calls instead of spam."
CA Alok S Kumar Founder, Alok S Kumar and Company (Chartered Accountants)
