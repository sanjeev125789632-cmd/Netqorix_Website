Local SEO Strategies for Engineering & B2B Services

Published Feb 10, 2026 · By Netqorix Engineering Team

Many professional service practices — chartered accountants, renewable energy installers, and architectural firms — assume SEO is exclusively for B2C e-commerce stores. In reality, high-intent local search queries frequently yield the highest-margin commercial contracts.

Google Business Profile Optimization

Claiming and verifying your Google Business Profile is the foundational step. Completing category selections, publishing geotagged project updates, and configuring structured service menus directly influences 3-pack Map rankings.

On-Page JSON-LD Schema Markup

Adding explicit LocalBusiness and ProfessionalService schema markup to your site enables search crawlers to verify your business address, phone number, operational hours, and price ranges without ambiguity.

SEO Compounds Over Time

SEO is a 3–6 month investment — rankings compound over time, delivering sustained organic inquiries long after initial technical setup.
